Getting Started
Set up in under five minutes.
1

Add Royal Guard to your server

Click "Add to Discord" and authorize the bot with the required permissions.

2

Bind your Roblox group

Run /groupbind to connect your Roblox group, then /rankbind to map ranks to Discord roles.

3

Set admin levels

Use /setadmin to give your staff team the right permission tier for your server.

4

You're live

Members verify with /verify and roles sync automatically. Run /help any time for the full command list.
